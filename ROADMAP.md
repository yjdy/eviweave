# Roadmap / 路线图

v0.1.0 was published on 2026-10-24. v0.1.1 is a maintenance candidate for preparation tools, outer daemon fixes, current-source CI and documentation. Actual publication status is recorded in the online Release.
v0.1.0 已于 2026-10-24 发布；v0.1.1 为准备工具、外层 daemon 修复、当前源码 CI 与文档维护候选，实际发布状态以在线 Release 为准。

- Review the frozen v0.1.1 cloud candidate, then separately authorize publication and anonymous acquisition checks. 审阅冻结的 v0.1.1 云端候选，再单独确认发布及匿名获取验证。
- Broaden platform and host support only after independent acceptance. 经过独立验收后再扩展平台和宿主支持。
- Design and certify complete online request/cost collection against G1 before enabling it. 完整在线请求/费用采集先设计并通过 G1，再启用。
- Develop EviWeave Controlled separately, preserving the V6 baseline and distinct experiment identities. Controlled is outside this maintenance release. 独立开发 Controlled，保护 V6 基线和实验身份；不在本维护版本范围。
- Scope a formal report, longer bilingual articles and PDF separately. New real experiments require explicit authorization, credentials and budget. 正式报告、中英文长文及 PDF 另定范围；新真实实验需明确授权、凭据和预算。
